# Popcorn Math Brief

**Source:** November 8, 2026 meeting with the client, Kashamar Lewis, and the owner, Abiodun.

## What it is
- Popcorn Math is an in-person, hands-on after-school maths-fun programme for primary school children on Anguilla. It is NOT an online learning product and NOT exam prep (that is the Readiness Check on Tutor Time). The point is that children enjoy maths by making something.
- Children from different primary grades (about grades 1 to 5) meet in one classroom for one hour each week. Examples of what they make: a 3D shape such as a tetrahedron or cube, and activities with manipulatives like popcorn. Every session ends with each child taking home something they made.
- A series runs about 8 to 10 weeks within a school term. Parents pay a fee per series (the amount is an owner decision: show no price). The first run is a pilot planned for January at the founder's own school, then the other schools (there are six primary schools on the island). Always say "planned", never "available now".

## How it works
- A presenter leads each session: takes attendance, hands out a packet of materials, shows the children how to make the item, and arrives with a finished model made by the founder. The presenter is the founder's "mini me".
- Presenters are independent presenters, adults aged 18 or over. The ideal presenter is a teacher at that school, so the classroom and child safeguarding are already in place and the teacher knows the children. Young adults (for example 18 to 19 year olds finishing school) are also welcome. Background checks are required (the formal vetting and code-of-conduct policy is still pending the owner's decision: say so internally, and on public pages say only that presenters are checked and that a code of conduct applies, as approved copy allows).
- Presenters are paid per session and receive a bonus if they attend every session. Attendance records are how payment is kept accurate. Do not put dollar amounts on the public site.
- Tutor Time is founder-led (Teacher Cassie). Popcorn Math uses independent presenters. Never blur the two.

## Who the site is for
- Parents: understand the programme, see when and where, register interest.
- Schools (principals, teachers): understand how a series runs and ask for a partnership.
- Presenters: the main recruitment channel. The founder will print flyers with a QR code. The QR code lands on the presenter page, which lists the required age, expectations, policies and how to apply. Candidates can also contact the team by email or WhatsApp.
- Children are not the main users. Keep the mini-game as a short fun teaser only.

## Brand and tone
- Brand name: Popcorn Math (no "s"). Colors: Popcorn Yellow #F5A623, Warm Kernel #F8E71C, Navy #1A365D, Plum #3D1E6D. Mascot: a popcorn bucket with arms and legs whose face changes expression. Fun, warm, trustworthy, simple English. Parents of young children will read this on phones.
- Domain: popcornmath.tutortime.ai (a subdomain of the main Tutor Time site). Main brand stays Tutor Time. The WhatsApp number is already configured.
